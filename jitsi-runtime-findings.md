## Moderator policy confirmed

Jitsi's official authentication announcement states that anonymous room creation on meet.jit.si was removed and an account is required for the person creating a room. The official user guide says that when the first participant connects to meet.jit.si, they are asked to authenticate or wait for a moderator. Therefore the screenshot is expected behavior from the public service, not a DSLMS/Supabase login failure.

The current `JitsiClassroom` component embeds public `meet.jit.si` with a deterministic room name and passes `userInfo.displayName`, but it has no Jitsi moderator credential/token. That means a teacher who is the first participant cannot be made moderator purely by React configuration. Students can join without accounts after a moderator has started the room.
